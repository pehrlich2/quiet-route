Company plan · Chittenden County, Vermont

Quiet Route Hauling

Electric trash, recycling and compost pickup for Jericho, Essex, Hinesburg, Richmond and nearby towns. It launches with a used electric pickup and a dump trailer, is funded by a founding-member campaign, and plans real routes from real addresses.

Business planCosts and a live cost calculator, pricing, composting, fundraising, when to commit, and what the company is worth. RoutingMap of real E911 addresses. Plans every-other-week routes with Williston dump runs, and checks summer and winter battery range. HardwareTow vehicle, the two trailer sizes and how to choose, the cart tipper (with drawings), GPS trackers and carts. SoftwareLive truck map, pickup notifications, and sign-up and billing built on Stripe. PoliciesWhat we take and don't, where carts go, missed pickups, our guarantee, billing and contamination. RegulatoryUSDOT, medical card, hours records, the CDL line, Vermont's hauler permit and recycling law, CSWD license, pricing rules. RisksTruck or trailer out of service, snow days, sick drivers, competition, and who to learn from.
