Appendix: why Williston, not Coventry

Coventry (near Newport) is Vermont's only landfill, ~75–80 miles from Burlington. Settled: we dump in Williston. The comparison, for the Silverado towing at ~1.2 kWh/mile:

WillistonCoventry
Round trip~16 mi, ~40 min~155 mi, 3.5–4 h
Energy, route + dump~90 kWh (2 runs)~235 kWh (1 run)
vs usable batteryfits winter (~140)over summer (~200)
Dump fee~$125/tonmaybe $20–40/ton less (est.)
Saved per ~1.75 t load—$35–70 for half a day

The county district fee and the $6/ton state tax apply either way.
